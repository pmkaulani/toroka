# Interview guide (50 minutes)

Read the consent text first. Start recording only after a clear yes. Do not lead: ask about the last time, not what they would do in general.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me a bit about yourself and how you use your phone for money and work.
- Probe: which apps and networks, who helps you with phone things.

## 2. Context: the last suspicious message (10 min)
- Think of the last message, call or offer that made you stop and wonder if it was real. What was it?
- What did you do next? Then what? Who did you tell or ask? How long until you decided?
- Probes: what made you doubt it; what would have made you sure; what did it cost you in time or money; would you do the same today?
- If they have never doubted a message, ask about a friend or relative who did.

## 3. Deep dive (20 min)
**A. How they check today**
- When you want to be sure, where do you look first? What do you trust, and why?
- Have you used 333, a caller-ID app, a search, a friend, a Facebook group, or an official website? What was good or frustrating?
- What makes it hard to check in the moment?

**B. Six-message behavioural test** (stimulus cards below, one at a time, in random order)
For each: "Real, fake, or not sure? How sure, from 1 to 5? What would you do next? What would you check first?"
Note the decision, confidence, the words or details they point to, and the next action.

**C. Categories** 
- Which of these do you get most: jobs or bursaries, shop or seller messages, M-PESA messages, loan offers, prizes, other?

## 4. Reaction (10 min)
Show three paper versions of the same result for card S3 (below). Only the unknown-state wording differs:
1. "Inconclusive."
2. "We couldn't confirm this either way."
3. "Not enough evidence yet. Here is what we checked and what we didn't."
- What does this tell you? What would you do next? Is it helpful, or is it a tool failing?
- Which wording do you trust most? Which would you show a friend?
- How would you want to send us a message: paste, share from your phone, forward to a number, or take a photo?

## 5. Wrap-up (5 min)
- Is there anything I should have asked?
- Would you be willing to share one real message with personal details removed? (Separate yes or no.)
- Referral: is there one person with a different experience I could talk to?
