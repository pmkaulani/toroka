      Digital Safety in Kenya

      Key safety rules to remember when transacting or communicating online in Kenya:

      • Never share your M-Pesa PIN, SIM card details, or one-time verification SMS (OTP) with anyone, including Safaricom customer care.
      • Official Safaricom communication only comes from official SMS sender IDs or phone number 0722 000 000.
      • Before paying a social-media seller, independently verify the seller and recipient, agree written delivery/refund terms, and understand any buyer protection. Be cautious of pressure or unexplained upfront fees; a deposit alone does not prove fraud.
      • If you accidentally sent money to a wrong number, immediately forward the transaction SMS to 456 to trigger an M-Pesa reversal.

      Emergency & Fraud Reporting Contacts

      • Safaricom Fraud: Forward suspicious SMS to 333 (free) or call 100/200.
      • DCI Kenya / Cyber Crime: Report online fraud at cyber@dci.go.ke or call 0800 722 203.
      • Communications Authority of Kenya (CAK / NC4): incidence@ke-cirt.go.ke.
