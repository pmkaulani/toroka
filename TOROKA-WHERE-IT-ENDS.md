# Where Toroka Ends — Product Destination

Toroka should not become an endless chain of numbered builds. The builds are engineering increments towards one cohesive product: a Kenya-first digital fraud investigation and consumer-protection platform.

## The finished user experience

A person opens Toroka without creating an account and submits any combination of:

- SMS, WhatsApp or email text;
- a website URL or shortened link;
- an Instagram, TikTok or Facebook shop/profile;
- a seller/business name or claimed registration;
- an M-PESA message or receipt screenshot;
- product photos, advertisements or a conversation/call transcript.

Toroka routes the material through the relevant checks, rather than running every expensive or intrusive check on every input.

It investigates:

1. The message, offer or payment request: urgency, upfront fees, impersonation, credential requests, inconsistencies and known patterns.
2. The web presence: URL safety, redirects, domain registration, DNS, hosting/network context, certificate observations and historical scan records.
3. The social seller: platform-authorized or user-supplied profile evidence, identity claims, public shop details and consistency with other sources.
4. The business: official BRS/NEA material where available, registration/licence status, name/domain/phone/address comparisons and physical-location candidates.
5. The campaign: repeated scripts, reused public advertisements/images, shared identifiers and related infrastructure—always with provenance, consent and review requirements.
6. The payment context: what a supplied message/receipt appears to say, whether amount/payee details are internally consistent, and what Toroka cannot independently confirm.

## What the user receives

One coherent investigator-style report:

- conclusion: high risk, suspicious, mixed evidence, lower observed risk, or insufficient evidence;
- the exact supporting observations and clickable original sources;
- source type, retrieval time, freshness and reliability limits;
- seller claims versus independently corroborated facts;
- related entities/campaign leads and why each relationship exists;
- contradictions, missing facts and what could not be checked;
- practical next steps, such as independently contacting the official business, not paying until the recipient is verified, preserving evidence, or reporting a suspected incident.

The product must never turn “not found in a threat feed” into “safe,” a map listing into “genuine,” a shared IP into “same owner,” or an allegation into a fact.

## Trust and governance

- Guest-first consumer access; optional accounts only for history, saved cases or professional workflows.
- Explicit consent for cross-case media matching; privacy minimization, retention and deletion controls.
- Moderator-reviewed public indicators and appeal/correction processes.
- Role-restricted private report details and immutable audit trails for sensitive access.
- Provider licensing and commercial-display rights reviewed before enabling each source.
- English and Kiswahili as supported product languages, with Sheng and additional languages evaluated separately before being advertised as reliable.
- Evaluation on consented, independently labelled Kenyan cases, with false-positive rates measured by category and language.

## Definition of done

Toroka is ready for a controlled public pilot when:

1. The normal chat route orchestrates all applicable Build 1–4 capabilities without duplicating or conflicting verdicts.
2. Live providers have approved credentials, commercial permissions, quotas and failure handling.
3. Security review covers SSRF, upload handling, authentication, abuse/rate limits, data encryption, backup/restore and incident response.
4. Privacy review covers Kenyan data-protection obligations, retention, consent, correction and deletion.
5. Moderator staffing and response processes exist for disputed reports and published indicators.
6. Real end-to-end browser tests cover URLs, social shops, screenshots, receipts and language variations.
7. Accuracy and calibration are measured on held-out, consented datasets, including legitimate sellers and ambiguous cases.
8. Production deployment has monitoring, alerts, backups, restore drills and a rollback plan.
9. A small Kenya pilot demonstrates user comprehension and safe decision support before wider release.

After that milestone, Toroka does not need a Build 5 just to keep adding features. It needs product operation: maintain providers, review evidence, improve the language/model based on labelled data, address false positives, and respond to user feedback. New builds should be driven by demonstrated gaps from the pilot.

## What Toroka is not

Toroka is not a law-enforcement database, a public blacklist of alleged scammers, a guarantee that a seller is legitimate, a substitute for official registry searches, or a way to look up private M-PESA accounts. It is an evidence-led decision-support and investigation product.
